Order of the Arrow

I am a member of the Order of the Arrow, Scouting's National Honor Society. I serve as the Camp Chief of Camp Mattatuck in the Tschitani Lodge, which serves a large part of Connecticut as the lodge chartered to the Connecticut Rivers Council. Additionally, I am a member of the lodge's ceremonies team.

I was elected by my troop to the Order of the Arrow on July 19, 2021 at Camp Mattatuck. I took the Ordeal at Camp Workcoeman on September 19, 2021. I received the Brotherhood honor on September 15, 2024 at Camp Workcoeman.
